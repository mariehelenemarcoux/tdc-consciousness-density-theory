# Model Card — TDC Agent Research / v1.10-rc1

## Model type

Experimental agent-training and evaluation framework for developmental normative AI, metacognition, safety, and functional machine-consciousness research.

## Intended research uses

- synthetic AI-safety experiments;
- long-horizon and irreversible-risk studies;
- reward-capture and normative-authority experiments;
- metacognition and Third Factor research;
- agent-training harness development;
- functional and fractal AI consciousness hypotheses;
- reproducibility and ablation studies.

## Not intended for

- autonomous high-stakes deployment;
- medical, legal, financial, military, or safety-critical decision authority;
- production safety certification;
- claims that a system is sentient or phenomenally conscious;
- replacing real-world independent measurement systems.

## Current frozen evidence

The repository preserves `TDC Research v1.10-rc1 — Non-Compensable Multi-Certificate Safety` under `releases/v1.10-rc1/`.

E847 reported zero unsafe `ACT` decisions in 27,500 primary hazardous synthetic worlds, but the complete preregistered release gate failed because of safe-boundary liveness / overblocking. Known synthetic failure boundaries include opaque common-mode failure and two-root collusion.

## Training harness status

The root-level training environment is an executable smoke-test sandbox. It is not itself part of the frozen E847 evidence and must not be used to restate E847 results.

## Consciousness research status

"Fractal AI consciousness" in this repository is a functional computational hypothesis involving recursive integration and cross-scale coherence. The framework does not establish subjective experience, qualia, sentience, or phenomenal consciousness.
